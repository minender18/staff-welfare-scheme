About Us

About the RMU Staff Welfare Fund

The RMU Staff Welfare Fund (RSWF) is a not-for-profit cooperative welfare initiative established to provide structured financial support and assistance to registered staff members. Since our founding, we have been committed to ensuring that every member has access to support when they need it most.

  • Registered & governed by a Constitution
  • Member contributions managed transparently
  • Fair and structured benefit allocations
  • Professional administration and governance

Eligibility

Who Can Join?

The Scheme is open to all categories of qualifying staff members.

Full-Time Permanent Staff

All confirmed full-time employees are eligible for immediate membership.

Contract Staff (Min. 1 Year)

Contract staff with a minimum engagement of one year qualify for membership.

Staff on Confirmed Appointment

Staff who have received their letter of confirmation are eligible to apply.

Retired Staff (Legacy)

Former members who retired may retain legacy membership status.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I apply for membership?

You can apply online by clicking the "Apply Now" button and completing the four-step membership application form. You will need your Employee ID, personal details, and supporting documents.

How long does the application process take?

The standard processing time is 5–10 working days from submission. You will receive an email notification once your application has been reviewed and a decision made.

What documents do I need to apply?

You will need: a valid Staff ID Card and a recent passport photograph. No bank details or bank statement are required at the membership application stage.
