About the RMU Staff Welfare Fund

Our history, mission, and commitment to every member.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, equitable, and transparent welfare support to all registered staff members, ensuring financial security and peace of mind for members and their families during times of need.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted, member-focused welfare cooperative in Ghana — a scheme where every member feels genuinely supported, valued, and heard in every aspect of their welfare journey.

Our Story

How We Started

The RMU Staff Welfare Fund was founded in 2012 by a small group of dedicated employees who recognised the need for a structured, member-driven welfare fund. What began as a grassroots initiative has grown into one of the most reputable staff welfare cooperatives in Ghana.

Over the years, we have grown our total assets to GH₵2.3 million, onboarded 215 active members, and expanded our benefit categories. Every decision we make is guided by our Constitution and our unwavering commitment to our members.

2012
Founded

RSWF was established by 42 founding members with three core benefit categories: medical, bereavement, and emergency loans.

2015
Expansion

Membership grew to 500+. Education bursary and emergency loan benefits were introduced.

2018
Constitution Ratified

A formal Constitution was ratified at the Annual General Meeting, establishing governance structures and member rights.

2021
Digital Transformation

Online application portal launched. Processing time reduced from 21 days to under 10 days for most applications.

2026
Today

215 active members, GH₵2.3M+ in total assets, 15+ benefit categories. Stronger than ever.

Governance

Constitution & Governance

The Scheme is governed by a ratified Constitution that outlines the rights and obligations of all members, the procedures for benefit applications, and the structure of the Board of Trustees.

All financial decisions are subject to Board approval and periodic review by an independent auditor. Members have the right to access audited accounts at any time.
